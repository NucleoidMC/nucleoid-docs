Implements scrolling behaviour to the regular [SidebarWidget](sidebar-widget.md) if it has more than 14 lines

---

- ScrollableSidebarWidget(int, Predicate&lt;ServerPlayer&gt;)
- ScrollableSidebarWidget(Component, int)

(int is the amount of ticks per line)
